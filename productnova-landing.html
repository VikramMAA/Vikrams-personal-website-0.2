The sprint

What the six weeks actually look like

Every Friday you get a build on a live URL. There's no big reveal at the end, because you've been using it since week two.

01
Week 0–1

Blueprint

Two working sessions. We pin down the one job your product has to do, cut everything that isn't v1, and give you a scope and a fixed price to sign off on.

02
Week 1–2

Design

A clickable prototype of the real screens, not a moodboard. You click through it and tell us what's wrong before a line of production code exists.

03
Week 2–5

Build

Frontend, backend, database, integrations and any AI in the product. Deployed continuously, so you're testing real features while we're still writing them.

04
Week 5–6

Launch

Load tested, secured, monitored and live on your cloud account. You get the repo, the documentation and a walkthrough with whoever takes it forward.
